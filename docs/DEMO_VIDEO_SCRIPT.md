## 🎙 Recording Tips for Best Submission Quality

1. **Browser Setup:**
   - Chrome or Edge in Fullscreen (`F11`).
   - Resolution: `1920x1080` (1080p).
   - Ensure the browser zoom is set to `100%`.
2. **Audio:**
   - Use a clear microphone with minimal background noise.
   - Speak in a calm, confident, professional cadence.
3. **Pacing:**
   - Let the map finish its smooth camera glide before clicking the next button or card.
   - Move the mouse cursor with intentional, deliberate motions.
